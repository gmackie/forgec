## Ownership

Knowledge identity/revision, classified content, publication/audience links and feedback evaluation.

## Composition

Required dependencies: artifact, specification, classification, publication, entitlement, evaluation.

Collaboration, search and notifications are optional adapters; no CMS layout engine.

## Independent acceptance

Article/SOP/training; exact content/spec/concept revision; qualified publication; entitlement denial; feedback targets correct version; historical retirement.
