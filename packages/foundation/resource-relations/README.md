# Resource relations

Foundation #79 separates ownership, control, custody and domain-defined relations between typed Party and ResourceSubject identities. Domain satellites own equipment, inventory and cloud-specific fields; no generic ownerId or polymorphic target pair exists. Namespaced relation kinds pin a SpecificationPin. Exact optional quantities carry explicit units and scope. Relations convey no authorization or legal conclusion.

Use ResourceRelations.registerKind, establish, handoff, end and asOf. Candidates are inert until a RelationCommit admits them. A handoff's single immutable commit atomically claims its predecessor and successor, ending the predecessor and starting the successor at one kernel-stamped knowledge instant. Competing handoffs leave exactly one committed transition; losing staged candidates remain inert. Distinct roots may coexist: uniqueness protects a chain, not exclusive custody across all roots or extents. Domains must define cardinality and exclusivity themselves.

Selectors require both validAt and knownAt. Valid intervals are half-open; knowledge is inclusive at commit.createdAt. Future ends apply only at their effective boundary. Backdated termination/handoff preserves the earlier knownAt view. Equal publication timestamps are all included together; there is no synthetic ordering or selectable intermediate view between tied commits. Ancestry remains explicit. Corrections here mean subsequently learned termination/handoff, not arbitrary rewriting of old validFrom or replacement of entire assertions. createdAt on candidates never establishes knowledge of a relation. Client-provided publication timestamps are not trusted.

Provenance requires a typed EvidenceSource, immutable source record, and sealed evidence containing at least one matching source+sourceRecord item. Source occurrence and evidence observations must precede publication; effective time may differ (including future effects). All support and endpoint reads are authorized before publication and revalidated on interpretation. Empty/unrelated seals fail. A failed publication may leave an inert candidate. Direct generated RelationCommit.create is a low-level fact operation; only helper-validated interpretation is authoritative.

Live selection is bounded to 100 candidates per subject, including unpublished candidates and losing handoffs, and 128 ancestry nodes. Overflow fails rather than truncating. Reads are not a transaction-wide snapshot; callers needing a frozen settlement view must provide closure. Hidden dependencies or terminal facts fail closed. Historical selection requires current read authority over the entire history, including later admissions and terminations: inaccessible or invalid later facts can therefore make an earlier knownAt query fail closed. Retries use durable command keys; normalized equivalent input is handled by kernel receipt normalization, while payload changes are rejected.

The consumer fixture independently exercises leased equipment owner/operator/custodian, consigned inventory controller/custodian, cloud host/operator/payer and a typed custody event. Tests run through foundationAdapters on memory, SQLite, and PostgreSQL when configured.

F79-SEMANTIC is deferred: this package's bounded valid/known interpretation is a seam for ConceptIR #75 and relationship facets #77; it does not implement those language features. F79-STORE remains planned until the integrated hosted adapter receipt covers this package. Local provider-compatible tests are not hosted deployment proof.
