# Contextual trust and reputation

Foundation system for #107. TrustSubject is an attachment point for typed Party, device or domain-owned subjects. TrustDimension binds a context, score scale and exact SpecificationPin method. There is no global reputation number. SignalKind is a domain vocabulary with its own specification; TrustSignal records issuer, source identity, observation time, explanation and sealed Evidence. Consumer payloads demonstrate vendor delivery lateness, marketplace resolution and verified agent runs without untyped JSON.

TrustReview freezes an explicit, bounded list of 1..128 distinct signals and per-member rationale, a subject/dimension, half-open observation window and dedicated EvaluationSet. EvaluationRun must be created after that review and use its exact method/set. TrustAssessment records the completed Evaluation, contextual score, confidence and explanation. The service verifies membership, source evidence, window, scale, subject and dimension. Domain evaluators execute the pinned aggregation/decay specification; the service does not pretend that an arbitrary score is a universal formula or independently recompute external methods.

SignalCorrection retains the original fact and names a later-recorded replacement or withdrawal. SignalDispute and DisputeResolution preserve the challenge history. As-of inspection marks assessments unusable while a selected signal is disputed, after an upheld dispute, or after correction/withdrawal. Dismissal restores usability if no independent correction applies. Old references never switch silently to replacements: publish a fresh review and evaluation. Knowledge is retained separately in createdAt timestamps; as-of usability uses declared effective times and the review/assessment recording boundary. Hidden correction/dispute/evidence facts fail closed. Quarantined evaluation results cannot confer trust authority.

RiskTrustInput links a separate RiskAssessment to a usable contextual TrustAssessment at the risk assessment instant. Its rationale records why the assessment is relevant; this link is provenance, not proof that the evaluator executed a particular algorithm. Trust and future-harm Risk remain distinct. Authorization can consume inspected assessment facts through its own policies; a score never grants authority itself.

Applications must authorize trusted publishers/evaluators and deny untrusted raw TrustReview/TrustAssessment/RiskTrustInput writes that bypass service checks. The system proves consistency and provenance of declared facts, not the honesty of observers or external algorithms. Generated row rules enforce method/set bindings, score ranges and immutable history. Tests cover memory, SQLite and PostgreSQL; live external provider certification is separate.

The Party mapping is `TrustPartySubject`, prefixed so this package can co-deploy
with Qualification's `PartySubject`. Consumers of the initial 0.1 symbol must update
that resource name; TrustSubject identity and assessment semantics are unchanged.
