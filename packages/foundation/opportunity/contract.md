# Opportunity and pursuit

A Foundation system for #103: a durable possible valuable outcome before commitment. The Opportunity owns identity, subject Party, ParticipationSet, qualification criteria, evaluation set, potential outcome and expected timing. Sales, partnership, grant and procurement consumers keep their typed domain details. Opportunity is not a ConceptIR kernel primitive, an offer, or an agreement.

PursuitDefinition pins a domain specification. Immutable stages carry domain labels and Open/Won/Lost/Abandoned outcomes; explicit StageTransition facts define allowed movement. OpportunityEvent preserves the ordered stage history, next action, actor, reason and optional assessment. Unique journal positions serialize conflicting transitions. Terminal outcomes cannot reopen; a new pursuit can carry domain-owned follow-up links. Journals are bounded to 128 events and fail closed at capacity.

OpportunityAssessment pins a completed Evaluation of this pursuit's qualification criteria and evaluation set. Optional decimal confidence and value/unit are the evaluator's sourced forecast, never guaranteed revenue or authoritative probability. Quarantined evaluations cannot qualify new transitions and make dependent inspection fail closed; the immutable records remain readable for historical audit. Application policies decide which evaluations warrant which stages: completion alone is not a positive verdict.

OpportunityQuote links a real Quote for the subject buyer. OpportunitySelection links a Solicitation using the pursuit's participants; it does not create or bypass the Selection decision process. Winning requires an issued active or scheduled Agreement for the subject customer, with issuance no later than the event's effective time. Quote acceptance and Agreement issuance remain owned by their existing services; retrying a failed pursuit transition never recreates the agreement. Conversion records provenance, not legal authority. Party membership alone grants no authorization.

Applications authorize commands and restrict untrusted direct assessment/event writes; service checks include authorized history replay, evaluation quarantine/evidence, and Agreement validation. Generated row rules enforce structural bindings, stage transitions, journal ordering and issued-agreement references. Raw facts do not substitute for those service checks. Relevant hidden history fails closed. Tests use memory, SQLite and PostgreSQL; no live provider execution is certified.
