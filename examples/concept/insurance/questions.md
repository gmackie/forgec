# Modeling pressure

1. Evidence digests preserve identity but do not prove authenticity, retention or chain of custody. Compare clinical source provenance and benefits notices.

2. Effective validity fields and asOf encode intent without establishing non-overlap, bitemporal corrections or correct coverage-version selection.

No new primitive is proposed. Apply relationship → facet → pattern → domain wrapper before kernel promotion; require cross-domain recurrence, English readability, substitution tests and a compiler consumer.
