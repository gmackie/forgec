# Modeling pressure

1. Cross-record quantity and money invariants cannot be expressed by Unique. Compare banking balancing and benefits retroactive corrections before proposing an invariant facet.

2. A workflow wait names an occurrence but cannot bind it to an order or prove compensation order. Correlation and exactly-once refund behavior remain unproven.

No new primitive is proposed. Apply relationship → facet → pattern → domain wrapper before kernel promotion; require cross-domain recurrence, English readability, substitution tests and a compiler consumer.
