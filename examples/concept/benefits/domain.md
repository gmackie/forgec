# Government benefits: eligibility and appeals

DetermineEligibility combines household composition, evidence and the rule version effective for the benefit period. IssueAward records those inputs so an applicant can understand the decision. Recertification runs on a schedule and can suspend future awards. HearAppeal waits for an independent reviewer; a successful appeal creates a retroactive adjustment rather than rewriting prior payments.

DeliverNotice exports a legally meaningful notice and waits for delivery evidence. Agency, case assignment and determination purpose restrict access. Valid time and recorded time may differ after a late correction. Storing dates and rule references is representable; proving which policy and household version was selected remains a gap.
