# Modeling pressure

1. Bitemporal rule/household corrections recur with banking and insurance. AsOf plus fields cannot prove temporal joins or non-overlapping validity intervals.

2. A notice delivery fact is not proof of legally adequate service. Provenance needs evidence obligations rather than a new universal Notice primitive.

No new primitive is proposed. Apply relationship → facet → pattern → domain wrapper before kernel promotion; require cross-domain recurrence, English readability, substitution tests and a compiler consumer.
