# Consensus service and verification seam

A small replicated register service accepts a client command identified by commandId, expected log index and value digest. ProposeCommand exports ClientCommand to ProtocolEngine. Only a committed certificate can reach ApplyCommitted, the sole logical owner of ReplicatedState and CommandCommitted. Three physical replicas do not imply three semantic producers: each realizes the same committed history. A partition can leave a client request pending; a timeout is not evidence that no commit occurred. A duplicate command must be correlated rather than applied twice.

The graph describes admission and evidence, not a consensus implementation. Term checks, quorum membership, election, retransmission, crash recovery and linearizability remain obligations of ProtocolEngine. An invalid certificate or stale index must be rejected by a future bridge. This corpus supplies no bridge and its null behavior fields do not enforce those checks. In particular, a declaration named CommitCertificate is not a cryptographic or logical proof.

A separate analysis workflow accepts VerificationRequest with exact model/config/tool digests. CheckProtocol exports it to TLC; a Completed event carries VerificationReport to RecordVerification. That process alone owns VerificationRun and its immutable VerificationRunRecorded receipt. An analyzer crash, timeout, violated invariant and successful bounded search must be distinct results; interrupted exploration cannot be admitted as passed. Result admission must match request/model/config/tool identity and retain any counterexample.

The supplied experiment isolates the reason majority voting helps: three voters, two values, one slot, each voter votes at most once. Decide may add a value only with a strict majority. It allows every order of votes/decisions and indefinite stuttering, but no message queues, elections or recovery. TLC explored 41 distinct states and found no violation of TypeOK, Agreement or Validity for this finite configuration. That is useful executable handoff evidence, not a production protocol proof.
